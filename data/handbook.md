# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (sample)

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval. Home-office stipend is $500 one-time, claimed via the Expenses portal within 60 days of hire.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next calendar year. PTO requests over 10 consecutive days need manager approval 30 days in advance.

## Security Policy
All laptops must use full-disk encryption. Passwords are managed through the company password manager; sharing credentials over chat is prohibited. Report suspected phishing to security@acme.example within one hour.

## Expense Policy
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Flights over $800 require pre-approval. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

## On-call
Engineers on the primary on-call rotation receive a $300 weekly stipend. The pager must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. Escalate to the secondary after 30 minutes without response.
